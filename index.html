how I work

I build agent-first.

Reliability used to be measured in how fast a human could be woken up. I think the better question is what should never need a page at all. So I design systems where agents hold the pager first: they investigate, they attribute, they revert, and they hand a human the one decision that actually needed judgment. Same SLOs — far fewer humans losing sleep over them.

  • 01 Agents that debug themselves. autoInvestigator root-causes trunk breakages and generates the reverting change — ~15% close with no engineer in the loop.
  • 02 Agents that run process. Incident and change “babysitters” shepherd risky changes through their whole lifecycle, returning ~24 engineer-hours a week.
  • 03 Harnesses, not prompts. A Claude Code harness for Meta’s recommendation systems took trunk-health SLO attainment from ~60% to 100% in five months.
  • 04 Shipped before it was a trend. LLM triage tooling at Synopsys in 2023 (LlamaIndex, vLLM, Hugging Face); agent scaffolding used by Ollama and the U.S. Air Force.
